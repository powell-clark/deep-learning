- [ ] **AC-4** — Explains knowledge distillation with its loss, and contrasts data and model parallelism
- [ ] **AC-5** — Trains with automatic mixed precision and reports the speed and memory difference against full precision
- [ ] **AC-6** — Applies dynamic quantisation to a trained model and reports size, latency and accuracy change
- [ ] **AC-7** — Distils a small CNN from a larger one and compares accuracy against training the small model directly
