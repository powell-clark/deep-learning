## Identity

A university-grade deep learning curriculum that teaches neural networks, transformers, and modern generative models through mathematical derivation and from-scratch implementation.

## Vision

A complete deep learning curriculum from the perceptron through transformers, pretraining, alignment and generative models — the same rigour and format as the supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning series.

## Mission

Derives every architecture and optimiser from first principles, implements them from scratch in NumPy, and reproduces them with PyTorch and Hugging Face — paired theory and practical notebooks per lesson, all Colab-runnable. This project vows to teach honestly — every derivation shown in full, every claim demonstrated by runnable code, every limitation stated plainly, and nothing published that has not executed cleanly end to end.
