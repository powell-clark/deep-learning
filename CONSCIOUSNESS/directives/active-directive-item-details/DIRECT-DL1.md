# DIRECT-DL1: Ship complete deep learning curriculum

## Vision
Deliver a complete, university-grade deep learning curriculum — from the perceptron through
transformers, pretraining, alignment, generative models and deployment — with the same rigour
and format as the supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning series. Every method is
derived from first principles, implemented from scratch in NumPy, and reproduced with PyTorch
or Hugging Face, across paired theory and practical notebooks that run in Google Colab.

## Scope
- 16 lessons (0–15), each a paired theory (`a`) + practical (`b`) notebook: 32 notebooks.
- 4 professional-practice notebooks (X1–X4): debugging, evaluation, deployment, research frontiers.
- Cross-cutting: Colab-runnable (FEAT-DL1), NumPy + PyTorch/HF dual implementation (FEAT-DL2),
  verified reproducibility under the deterministic verifier (FEAT-DL3).

## Success Metrics
- Comprehensiveness: classical foundations through current practice (transformers, alignment, diffusion).
- Accessibility: every architecture derived from first principles, no step assumed.
- Practicality: every notebook runs on free Colab CPU in under 10 minutes.
- Verifiability: every notebook passes the deterministic verifier before its task closes.
